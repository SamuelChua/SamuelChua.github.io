Hello! My name is **Samuel Chua**, and I am a Robotics Masters student in the [GRASP Lab](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/). I am advised by [Vijay Kumar](https://www.kumarrobotics.org/) and [Camillo J. Taylor](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~cjtaylor/home.html). I believe in exploring the unknown and very much enjoy the thrill of learning through curiosity!

My research interests are in **Perception-based Autonomy**, Semantic Reasoning and Foundational Models for robots.

I was a SDE intern at Amazon with the Foundational People Data Services (FPDS) team and worked on a centralized AWS DynamoDB data transformation mechanism to generate real-time data representations.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science & Engineering from UCLA in 2025. I worked with Professor [Brett Lopez](https://btlopez.github.io/) in [VECTR Lab](https://vectr-ucla.github.io/) on SLAM and change detection and with Professor [Ankur Mehta](https://uclalemur.com/people/ankur-mehta/) in [LEMUR Lab](https://uclalemur.com/) on Deep Reinforcement Learning. I had multiple internship stints with [DSO National Laboratories](https://www.dso.org.sg/), particularly with the Robotics Autonomy Lab led by [Lionel Heng](https://www.lionel.work/), where I worked on Degeneracy-aware LiDAR-Inertial Odometry and Viewpoint Generation.
